🔍 What Is Offline Voice Typing?

Imagine typing by simply speaking - without the internet hearing you. That's exactly what Offline Voice Typing does. It's a powerful speech-to-text application that runs entirely on your Windows computer, transforming spoken words into written text without ever sending your voice to any server.

Whether you're a writer, student, professional, or someone who wants to give their hands a break, this tool works for you. It's like having a personal assistant that writes everything you say - securely and instantly.

🛠️ System Requirements

The application is designed to be light and efficient. Here's what you need:

  • 🗔 Windows Operating System: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit recommended).
  • 💾 Storage Space: At least 2 GB of free disk space for language models.
  • 📀 RAM (Memory): 4 GB minimum, 8 GB recommended for smooth performance.
  • 🎤 Microphone: Any built-in or USB microphone that works with Windows.
